About Rahulx.com

A one-person publication built on a simple bet: the future stops being intimidating the moment someone explains it plainly.

Who's behind this

I'm Rahul, an independent writer and developer, and Rahulx.com is my personal project — part magazine, part workshop. I research and write everything here myself, and I build the tools myself, which is why the site has the slightly obsessive, tinkerer's-lab feel it does: mismatched topics, working code, and a running curiosity about where technology and culture are headed next.

What you'll find here

Rahulx.com is organized into topic hubs rather than a single blog feed, because the subjects don't really belong on the same shelf:

  • AI & Robotics — how modern AI systems actually work, where robotics is heading, and the tools reshaping both fields.
  • Technology — everything in consumer and infrastructure tech that isn't strictly AI: hardware, software trends, the web, and how it all fits together.
  • Coding — practical programming knowledge, languages, and the craft of building software.
  • Space — missions, discoveries, and the physics of getting off this planet.
  • Oceans — the least-explored 71% of Earth, and why it matters as much as space does.
  • Ethics — the harder questions technology keeps raising, examined from multiple angles rather than one.
  • Political Ideologies — neutral, comparative explainers of the belief systems that shape policy debates worldwide.
  • Gaming, Formula 1, and Netflix & Streaming — the entertainment side, covered with the same curiosity.
  • Trading & Markets and How to Succeed — practical frameworks for money and personal growth, written as education, not advice.
  • Body Hacks and Paranormal — evidence-based self-improvement science, and a skeptical, curious look at unexplained phenomena.
  • UPSC CSE — a dedicated hub for India's Civil Services Examination aspirants.
  • Tools — a growing set of free, no-signup calculators and generators: tax and interest calculators, a BMI tool, a debt payoff planner, character counters, logo and dice generators, and more, all built to run instantly in your browser.

Why the sci-fi aesthetic

The visual style — dark interfaces, HUD panels, glowing telemetry — isn't decoration for its own sake. It's a running theme: most of what we cover here (AI, space, robotics, even the future of money) would have sounded like science fiction a generation ago. The design is a small reminder that we're already living in someone's "future," and it's worth understanding rather than just scrolling past.

How this site is built and funded

Rahulx.com is hand-built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no ad-heavy templates, no autoplay, no dark patterns. It's supported by display advertising (via Google AdSense) and, eventually, reader support, which keeps the articles and every tool completely free to use. Advertising never influences the opinions or rankings published here — see the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy for the specifics of how ads and data are handled.

Editorial approach

Articles here are written to be understandable on a first read, sourced from primary material and reputable reporting where possible, and updated when facts change — especially on fast-moving topics like AI tools and market conditions. On genuinely contested subjects (ethics, political ideologies), the goal is to lay out the strongest version of each position rather than pick a side for you.
