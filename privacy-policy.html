Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026. This page explains what information Rahulx.com collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Rahulx.com ("we," "our," "the site") is an independently run publication covering artificial intelligence, technology, coding, space, gaming, trading, entertainment, and related subjects, along with a set of free online tools. The site is operated by Rahul. For any privacy question not answered here, use the Contact page.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

If you email us or use the contact form, we receive whatever you choose to include — typically your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to reply to you.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our server and any analytics or advertising scripts we run may automatically log standard technical data when you visit, such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed, and time spent on the site. This is aggregate, non-precise data used to understand traffic and improve the site — it is not used to identify you personally.

2.3 Tools that process input locally

Utilities on this site — calculators, converters, generators, and similar tools under /pages/main_pages/tools/ — run entirely in your browser. Numbers or text you type into them (for example a tax calculation or a BMI value) are processed on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our advertising and analytics partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

  • Remember basic preferences (such as menu state) between visits.
  • Measure aggregate traffic and understand which content is useful.
  • Serve and measure advertising, including frequency capping and fraud prevention.

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site functionality but will not prevent you from reading content.

4. Advertising

Rahulx.com works with third-party advertising networks to display advertising and help fund the free content and tools on this site. As part of that relationship:

  • Our advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising from participating vendors by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices, or by adjusting your browser's cookie and ad-personalization settings directly.
  • Third-party vendors may show our ads on sites across the internet based on prior visits to this and other websites.
Note: We do not control the specific ads served by our advertising partners' networks, and we do not see or store the personal data those systems use to select those ads.

5. Children's privacy

Rahulx.com is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited technical data with service providers strictly to operate the site (for example, our hosting provider) and with advertising/analytics partners as described above. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of the site, its users, or the public.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Because Rahulx.com collects very little personal data directly (mainly what you send us by email), most such requests can be handled quickly — reach out via the Contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. External links

Our content links to third-party sites (news sources, tools, videos, social profiles, and similar). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites — please review their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Contact page.
