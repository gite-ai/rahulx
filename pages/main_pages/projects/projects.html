ToDoX

A privacy-first to-do list and task manager for Android. Tasks, sub-step checklists, project categories, deadlines, and repeat schedules all work fully offline in Guest Mode — nothing leaves your device unless you choose to sign in. Signing in with Google adds real-time sync across devices via Firebase, plus encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.

Platform: Android Package: com.rx.todox No ads, no third-party tracking

Built to answer a simple frustration: most task apps either lock features behind a paywall or quietly harvest data. ToDoX keeps the core experience fully offline-capable, and the moment cloud sync is not something you want, it stays entirely out of the picture.
